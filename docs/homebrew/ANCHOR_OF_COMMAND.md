# Anchor of Command

**Feat**

**Prerequisite:** None

Increase your **Constitution or Charisma by 1**, to a maximum of 20.

While you are not incapacitated:

- You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to resist being shoved, dragged, pushed, pulled, or otherwise moved against your will.
- Allies within **5 feet** of you gain **+1 AC** against attacks from creatures you can see.

## Hold Fast

Once per Long Rest, as a **Bonus Action**, you plant yourself in place until the start of your next turn.

Until then:

- Your speed becomes **0**.
- You cannot be moved against your will.
- When you activate Hold Fast, allies of your choice within **10 feet** gain temporary hit points equal to:

`proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier`

- Allies within **10 feet** of you gain **+1 AC** until the effect ends.

## Grimoire Representation

Automate where supported:

- +1 Constitution or Charisma choice
- 1/Long Rest resource
- Bonus Action activation
- speed becomes 0
- temporary hit point formula
- temporary +1 AC effect

Potentially table-resolved:

- adjacency
- exact forced-movement immunity if positioning is not modeled
