## 3. Why I built it
I built Grimoire because I wanted to create my character, not the character an app allowed me to create.

That frustration became one rule: your character should not be limited by your character sheet app. Homebrew goes through the same systems as the built-in content, the table can change the rules, and a player or DM can override what the software cannot anticipate.

I am not asking you to promote it, make a video, endorse it or mention it to anyone. I am not asking to redistribute your homebrew either. Use one of your own designs, privately, as a stress test, and it stays yours.

I do not want promotion. I want you to find where this idea fails in practice.

## What you would get if it works
If it works for your designs, it could be a way to hand your own work to your own players as one file, with everything it depends on included, if you ever wanted that. That is entirely your choice and I am not asking for it now. They need the app, and for now it is Android only.
