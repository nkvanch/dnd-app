# Grimoire homebrew system: current limitations

A standalone, evidence-backed summary of what Grimoire's homebrew and content system cannot do today, drawn from a full investigation across every content type (classes, subclasses, races, spells, items, monsters, and full character builds). Nothing here is built or changed, this is analysis only, as of 22 September 2026.

Every point below cites the exact file and line checked to confirm it, so this document is self-contained. [HOMEBREW_AUTHORING_LIMITS.md](HOMEBREW_AUTHORING_LIMITS.md) and [CANONICAL_STRESS_SUITE.md](CANONICAL_STRESS_SUITE.md) carry the full reasoning and the specific stress-test cases that surfaced each one, for anyone who wants more than the citation.

## Missing primitives

These need real engine work. Nothing currently in the content model supports them.

1. **Modes and forms.** A character, monster, or item cannot hold more than one independent "which of several states am I in" at a time if two of those states both want to fully replace the stat block. The one real transformation mechanism (`transform` effect, `src/engine/types.ts:2072`) writes to a single optional field, `wildShapeState`, on `Entity` — not a keyed collection. It is layered onto the resolution pipeline the same non-mutating way `DmOverride` is (`src/engine/pipeline.ts`), so two designs that both want that slot (a racial form and an active transformation spell, say) collide on it directly. A stance-style mode built on conditions (`entity.conditions`, a real list) does not have this problem — conditions already stack independently today.
2. **Level-gated features beyond character level 1.** Any racial trait or ability meant to unlock later as the character levels up does not actually activate. Not a guess: over a dozen official races carry the literal code comment *"this app doesn't yet support level-gated racial features"* (`src/content/races/index.ts:32`, `:1012`, `:1553`, and more). It is not spell-specific either — official Aasimar's own level-3 Celestial Revelation transformation ships with the prompt *"unlocks at 3rd level, reference only"* and every one of its three options has an empty `effects: []` (`src/content/races/index.ts:2443-2470`).
3. **No re-selectable choice.** Once a `ChoiceDefinition` resolves (`resolved: boolean`, `src/engine/types.ts:1073,1109`), nothing in `src/engine/leveling.ts` or `src/engine/choiceEligibility.ts` lets it be swapped for a different option later — the only `swap*` function found, `swapBackground`, is unrelated. Warlock-style "change one invocation when you level up" designs are not supported.
4. **No choice dependency.** `ChoiceDefinition` (`src/engine/types.ts:1064`) has no field for "this choice's options depend on how an earlier choice resolved" — checked every field on the type, nothing like a `dependsOn` or prerequisite-choice-id exists. Picking a draconic ancestry color and then only seeing that color's matching options cannot be expressed as data.
5. **No point-budget choice.** `ChoiceDefinition.count` (`src/engine/types.ts:1064`) is always a fixed number of picks. The only point-budget mechanism anywhere is `PointBuyConfig`, hardcoded to ability-score generation (`src/engine/pointBuy.ts`), not a general-purpose choice type. A build-your-own-lineage system spending a shared trait-point budget across variously-costed options has nothing to use.
6. **No persistent state pointing at another creature.** `targetId` exists (`GrantResult`, `src/engine/types.ts:157`; a combat-log event, `:2381`) but both are one-shot records of what a grant or logged action pointed at, not a durable reference a sheet keeps to another entity. A "Rival" or a maintained curse on a marked enemy has nothing to attach to. Confirmed failing three unrelated designs (Emperor Warlock's Rival, Napoleon's Chosen Rival, LaserLlama's Ranger's Quarry).
7. **No modifying something already granted.** Every grant type in `src/engine/types.ts`'s grant/effect union only adds something new — none change a property of something already granted. Confirmed on official content too, not just theory: Versatile weapons store their alternate damage as a plain string (`properties: ['versatile (1d8)']`, `src/content/items/index.ts:80`), not a real switchable die, and official Dragon Slayer weapons ship with `effects: []`, empty, despite their description promising bonus damage.
8. **No summoned or linked creature that the game state actually tracks.** `CompanionTemplate` (`src/engine/companion.ts:25`) is wired only through a hardcoded map from a specific granting feature id to one of three official templates — not authorable by homebrew. Worse for spells: all 12 official "Summon X" spells already in `src/content/spells/generated.ts` (`summon_fey`, `summon_beast`, `summon_undead`, etc.) are placeholder text, literally `"description": "Description not available (not OGL)... Summon 1 fey spirit... friendly (stat block/your lvl)."`, with zero effect data.
9. **No monster phases.** `MonsterTemplate` (`src/content/monsters/types.ts`) has exactly one `stats`, one `ac`, one `speed`, one `features` list. There is no field for a second phase, and no HP-threshold trigger to switch into one anyway (see next point).
10. **No HP-threshold trigger of any kind.** "Bloodied" only controls how HP is displayed to players (`src/engine/houseRules.ts:196-205`) — it never fires an effect when a creature crosses it.
11. **No swarm or unit abstraction.** Searched the whole engine and the monster type for "swarm" — zero matches anywhere. A swarm-flavored monster today is an ordinary `MonsterTemplate` with descriptive text; nothing computes or enforces anything different about it.
12. **No real conditional recharge.** A monster or item's `recharge` field only supports `'short_rest' | 'long_rest' | 'dawn' | 'never'` or a freeform display string. "Recharge on a 5 or 6" can be typed in, but nothing rolls a d6 or evaluates it.
13. **No item-level mode.** `Item` (`src/engine/types.ts`) has no mode, state, or charges field at all. A weapon that switches between configurations has no mechanism to change its own properties.
14. **No forced movement toward a point or object.** Searched the whole `Effect` union specifically for this — nothing resembling forced movement toward a location or another entity exists. An ability that pulls its wielder to a thrown weapon is not supported.
15. **No automatic detection of a fact about a target.** `Effect.situational?: { id, question }` (`src/engine/types.ts:1383`) is real and genuinely consumed (`Entity.situationalAnswers`, checked in `src/engine/pipeline.ts:432`), but it always needs a player to answer a yes-or-no question by hand — it never infers a fact like "this attacker is a fiend" from game state on its own.
16. **No cross-character resource sharing.** Searched for any mechanism to give or transfer a resource, use, or die to another character's sheet — none exists. Every `ResourceCost` and resource pool lives on one character only.
17. **No monthly or calendar trigger.** There is no in-app concept of an in-game month. `subclassId` is a single field set once (`src/engine/types.ts:746,762`), never re-rolled or swapped on any recurring trigger.
18. **No bulk or raw-JSON homebrew authoring.** Every homebrew entry is built one field at a time through its builder screen. JSON only appears on the export/import side of content that is already built (`.grimoire-pack`, character files), never as a way to author new content directly.

## Real mechanism, but nothing can author it

Cheaper to fix than the list above: the engine already has the capability, but the builder screens creators actually use never expose it.

- **Item charges.** An item's `Feature.activation.resourceCost` uses the exact same shared `resourceId` mechanism already proven working for Rage — the data model has no problem with it. The homebrew item builder hardcodes `resourceCost: null` when constructing a feature's activation (`app/homebrew/item-builder.tsx:279`) and never exposes it as an editable field.
- **Spell concentration effects.** `Spell.onConcentrationFeatures?: Feature[]` is real and genuinely consumed (`src/engine/combat.ts:307-316`, not decorative). The homebrew spell builder has no UI to author it at all — its own code comment says so outright: *"this builder currently has no UI to AUTHOR onConcentrationFeatures at all... the same honest 'mechanism built, no content exercises it yet' situation"* (`app/homebrew/spell-builder.tsx:150-154`).
- **Homebrew companions.** `CompanionTemplate` exists and works, but only for the three official templates (Steel Defender, Eldritch Cannon, Ranger's Companion, hardcoded in `src/content/companions/index.ts`) — there is no builder screen anywhere in `app/homebrew/` for a creator to define a new one.
- **Homebrew transformation forms.** The `transform` effect works and is not limited to any one class (see item 1 above). `ALL_BEAST_FORMS` (`src/content/beastforms/index.ts`) is a fixed 7-entry official list, with no homebrew builder and no merge point in `src/store/homebrewStore.ts`.

## What isn't a limitation

Checked and confirmed working, not assumed, so a future review doesn't waste time re-checking these:

- **Overlapping grants and override precedence.** `recomputeDerived` (`src/engine/pipeline.ts:132`) recomputes everything from scratch on every call via `collectAllEffects`, which re-gathers active features and equipped items fresh each time rather than mutating a running total — a proficiency granted by two independent sources naturally survives removing one. DM overrides are applied last and win over everything else, per the resolution code's own comment.
- **Shared resource pools with different costs per ability.** `resourceId` is a plain shared string key — Rage (`resourceId: 'rage_pool'`, `src/content/classes/index.ts:409,413`) is spent by one activation and upgraded by another at a later level. Two homebrew activations sharing one `resourceId` with different `quantity` values already work correctly.
- **Legendary and lair actions.** Checked on the actual SRD Lich: each legendary action is its own feature drawing from one shared `legendary_actions` pool at different costs, and `recharge: 'start_of_turn'` is genuinely processed (`src/engine/combat.ts:91`). `lairActions?: Feature[]` on `MonsterTemplate` is a real, structured field, not text.
- **A conditional effect gated on a yes-or-no fact.** `Effect.situational` (above) already works for "while in sunlight" or "while underwater" style conditions.
- **Armor replacing the AC formula.** `base_ac_formula` is a real `StrategyKind` (`src/engine/types.ts:134`) and is wired into the homebrew item builder (`app/homebrew/item-builder.tsx:261`).
- **Powers not being forced through the spell system.** `Feature.activation` and `Feature.abilityEffects` are the general-purpose mechanism every content type already uses — a psionic-style power can be an ordinary Feature with its own resource cost, no need to route through `Spell`.
- **Companion actions on the owner's sheet.** `CompanionSection.tsx` renders real `companion.actionCards` with working `Pressable` triggers, not just a status display.
- **Attack, save, and damage auto-resolution — disclosed, not a gap in this investigation.** Grimoire computes the numbers a feature produces but never rolls dice or resolves outcomes; this is an existing, disclosed design decision that applies equally to every class, not something new found here.

> **Update (engine unblock branch):** item charges, ally-targeting effects, mid-campaign tiered grants, mode groups, dice recharge amounts, threshold effects and death triggers are now implemented — see [ENGINE_UNBLOCK_EMPEROR_WARLOCK.md](ENGINE_UNBLOCK_EMPEROR_WARLOCK.md).
