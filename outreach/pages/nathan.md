## 3. What I am trying to achieve
The table decides what is valid. Grimoire tracks and calculates. Homebrew is not second-class content. Rules should be configurable when possible. Manual intervention should stay possible when the software cannot anticipate the table.

## What I would like you to challenge
- Which of these ideas have I implemented badly?
- What assumptions about homebrew design have I missed?
- Where would an experienced DM expect more freedom?

It does not have to crash the app. "This builder assumes designers always structure features this way", "a DM should be able to override this" and "this type of mechanic has nowhere sensible to live" are exactly the feedback I am after. I am not asking for any public mention.

Try one or two of your own pieces, that is all.

## What you would get if it works
An easy way to give a DM or a table your homebrew as one file with everything it needs. Because the table decides what is possible, they can change whatever does not fit their game. They need the app, and for now it is Android only.

## What I would most like back
Short answers are fine.

1. What could you not represent at all?
2. What only worked through an awkward workaround?
3. What did Grimoire model differently from how you naturally think about the mechanic?
4. What took much longer to build than it should have?
