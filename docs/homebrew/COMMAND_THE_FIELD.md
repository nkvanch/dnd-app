# Command the Field

**3rd-level enchantment**

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose up to **three willing creatures** you can see within range.

When you cast the spell, and again at the start of each affected creature’s turn while the spell lasts, that creature chooses one of the following benefits.

The chosen benefit lasts until the start of that creature’s next turn.

## Advance

The creature’s speed increases by **10 feet**.

## Brace

The creature gains **+1 AC**.

## Press

The first weapon attack the creature hits with before the start of its next turn deals an extra **1d6 damage**.

## Withdraw

The creature’s movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

A creature can benefit from only **one option at a time**.

## At Higher Levels

When cast using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target **one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd**.

## Grimoire Representation

This spell is designed to test:

- concentration
- three initial targets
- per-target mode selection
- mode reselection every turn
- +10 speed
- +1 AC
- conditional +1d6 weapon damage
- opportunity-attack suppression
- upcast target scaling
- effect expiration

Potentially reminder-assisted:

- Withdraw, if opportunity attacks are not directly modeled
- per-target mode switching if active mode groups are not yet fully supported
