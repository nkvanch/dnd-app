# Grimoire — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE — fill in when published]*

## The short version

Grimoire doesn't collect, transmit, or store any of your data anywhere
except on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no servers operated by the developer that your data ever
touches.

## What Grimoire does with your data

**Characters, campaigns, and homebrew content.** Everything you create in
Grimoire — characters, homebrew races/items/spells, campaign notes — is
stored locally on your device using on-device storage. It is never uploaded
anywhere. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, this data is gone;
there is no cloud backup, because there is no cloud.

**Camera.** Grimoire requests camera access solely to scan QR codes when
joining a campaign hosted by another player at your table. The camera feed
is never recorded, stored, or transmitted — it's used live, in the moment,
purely to read a QR code, the same way any QR scanner works.

**Campaign sync (Wi-Fi).** When you host or join a campaign, Grimoire
creates a direct connection between devices on the same local Wi-Fi network
— phone to phone, at your table. This does not use the internet and does
not pass through any server. Nothing about your campaign, characters, or
session ever leaves the room.

**No accounts.** Grimoire does not ask you to create an account, sign in,
or provide any personal information (name, email, etc.) to use the app.

## What Grimoire does NOT do

- Does not collect analytics or usage data of any kind.
- Does not include advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
- Does not transmit data to the developer, to Anthropic, to Google, or to
  any third party.
- Does not use your location. (Grimoire previously required a location
  permission as a side effect of an underlying networking library; this
  was removed by switching to a different library that doesn't need it.)

## Third-party services

Grimoire does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or
data-collection services. The only "third party" involved in Grimoire's
operation at all is the Google Play Store itself (for distributing and
updating the app), which has its own separate privacy policy governing the
install/update process, unrelated to anything Grimoire itself does.

## Children's privacy

Grimoire does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children, because it does not collect information from anyone, period. The
app has no age gate because there's nothing to gate — there's no data
collection to consent to.

## Changes to this policy

If Grimoire's data practices ever change (for example, if a future feature
introduces optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated first, and the
change will be clearly described here before it ships.
