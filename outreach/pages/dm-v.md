# Grimoire, for DM V

Your character should not be limited by your character sheet app.

15 to 30 minutes with one difficult piece of your own content is plenty.

## 1. Watch first
[DEMO LINK, 60 seconds: an unusual concept, a custom subclass and feature, testing it, and the resulting sheet]

## 2. Try it
Android build, free and open source, works offline and needs no account or external server: [DOWNLOAD LINK]
Install steps, about two minutes: [INSTALL LINK]

## 3. What I would like you to test
One question: can Grimoire represent the character you intended, instead of making you redesign it around the software?

Take one of your more unusual character designs, ideally one that mixes a resource, a custom progression, a condition, a passive effect and an active ability, and try to build it. Tell me where you had to:

- change the mechanic because Grimoire could not express it,
- fake it through an unrelated feature,
- leave part of it as plain text because nothing could model it,
- or fight the workflow.

A list of failures is the most useful thing you can send. I am not asking for any public mention.

## What you would get if it works
You could send a character concept to your players as one file. They import it, and their character sheet does the calculations from it without extra hassle. They need the app, and for now it is Android only.

## What I would most like back
Short answers are fine.

1. What could you not represent at all?
2. What only worked through an awkward workaround?
3. What did Grimoire model differently from how you naturally think about the mechanic?
4. What took much longer to build than it should have?

## 4. Optional sample
understudy-test-pack.grimoire-pack is a small, original, deliberately awkward character concept: a limited "Borrowed Roles" resource, a three stage custom progression, a passive AC modifier, an active ability that applies a condition, and that condition. It is there so you can see what a pack feels like before building your own. Import it from the Homebrew tab. Everything in it is labelled (Demo).
