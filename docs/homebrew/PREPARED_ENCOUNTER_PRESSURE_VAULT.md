# Prepared Encounter — The Pressure Vault

**Purpose:** DM / Host / Live-Session Demo Encounter  
**Recommended Party:** 4 characters, roughly levels 6–8  
**Primary Monster:** Glassback (CR 7)  
**Encounter Type:** Exploration → environmental tension → combat → mid-campaign reward opportunity

---

# Encounter Summary

Deep beneath an abandoned mining complex lies a sealed high-pressure cavern known locally as **the Pressure Vault**.

Miners once broke into the chamber while following an unusually rich metal vein. They found smooth tunnels, shattered tools, stripped ore deposits, and several hollow mineral shells they mistook for geological formations.

One shell was not dead.

A living **Glassback** has followed the exposed ore vein upward into the mine. At ordinary pressure it is increasingly unstable, confused, and desperate to reach deeper ground.

The creature is not evil and is not hunting the party for food.

It is seeking:

- metal
- salt
- crystal
- ore
- deeper pressure

The encounter should reward players who realize this.

---

# DM Setup

Prepare before the session:

- one Glassback
- one environmental hazard effect
- one public effect
- one secret effect
- one DM-only note
- one optional reward: Weight of Authority — Tier I
- one optional Standard of the Unyielding Line in the room as recoverable treasure or prior expedition gear

Do **not** activate the encounter automatically when the session begins.

The DM should explicitly activate it when the party enters the Pressure Vault.

---

# Scene 1 — The Descent

The party enters an abandoned mine section where:

- walls become unnaturally smooth;
- metal fittings have been chewed away;
- salt deposits are missing;
- crystal seams have been scraped clean;
- broken tools are left behind with the wooden handles intact;
- the deeper passages are damp with mineral-rich brine.

A character inspecting the area can infer that whatever passed through here prefers mineral material over flesh.

Useful clues:

- stripped ore veins;
- gouges shaped like grinding plates rather than teeth;
- no blood;
- discarded leather and wood;
- heavily damaged metal.

The DM should not immediately explain the Glassback's biology.

---

# Scene 2 — Pressure Warning

Before combat, apply the public environmental effect:

## Public Effect — Unstable Pressure

**Visible to all players**

The Pressure Vault is partially depressurized because of mining damage.

While inside the chamber:

- creatures hear periodic stone groans and sharp mineral cracking;
- loose objects vibrate;
- movement through fractured ground is difficult.

Mechanical effect:

- the central fractured zone is difficult terrain;
- creatures that Dash through the fractured zone must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity check or fall prone.

This effect is public and should appear openly in the session.

---

# Secret Effect — Resonant Fracture

**DM-visible only**

The mine roof is close to structural failure.

Whenever any creature deals **10 or more thunder damage** in the chamber, or a large explosive/bludgeoning impact occurs at the DM's discretion, increase the hidden **Collapse Counter** by 1.

At **3 Collapse**, the ceiling partially fails.

## Partial Collapse

Each creature in the chamber makes a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw.

On failure:

- 2d10 bludgeoning damage;
- knocked prone;
- one random 10-foot area becomes blocked by rubble.

On success:

- half damage;
- not knocked prone.

The Glassback is not automatically immune to this.

This effect remains secret until it triggers or players discover the instability through investigation.

---

# DM-Only Note — What the Glassback Actually Wants

The Glassback is trying to reach the deeper eastern tunnel.

It attacks primarily because the party is between it and that route or because they carry rich mineral equipment.

Target priority:

1. exposed ore, crystal, salt, or metal stores;
2. heavily armored creatures;
3. creatures actively blocking the deeper tunnel;
4. creatures that have seriously injured it;
5. lightly equipped creatures only when necessary.

If a character drops or throws a significant quantity of metal, salt, crystal, or ore away from the party, the Glassback may spend its next turn investigating or consuming it instead of attacking.

If given a clear route toward deeper pressure, the Glassback attempts to retreat rather than fight to the death.

This should **not** be shown to players unless they infer it through play.

---

# Encounter Activation

When the DM activates the prepared encounter:

1. Place the Glassback near the far mineral wall.
2. Set its starting Pressure based on the current chamber:
   - **2 Pressure** by default.
3. Apply **Unstable Pressure** publicly.
4. Activate **Resonant Fracture** secretly.
5. Keep the DM-only motive note visible only to the DM.

---

# Glassback Combat Behavior

Use the Glassback's normal stat block.

Suggested first rounds:

## Round 1

The Glassback identifies the richest mineral target.

If a heavily armored character is present, it likely closes with that character.

If two or more creatures line up, it may use Abrasive Jet.

## Round 2

If the party blocks the eastern tunnel, it attempts to create space using Forelimb Crush.

If its Pressure has fallen, it must decide whether to:

- Compress;
- keep fighting;
- retreat toward the deeper tunnel.

## Low HP

Below roughly one-third HP, the Glassback prioritizes escape unless trapped.

It should not behave like a suicidal boss monster.

# Optional Interaction — Braced

If a character becomes **Braced** during the encounter:

- show the AC increase;
- show the speed reduction;
- show forced-movement advantage;
- disable Dash where supported.

This creates a clean live example of a custom effect during combat.

A DM may apply Braced manually as a temporary ruling/effect even if the character does not normally have access to the demo `Take a Brace` action.

---

# Optional Interaction — Standard of the Unyielding Line

The Pressure Vault contains an old expedition standard wedged into a reinforced staging platform.

If the DM wants the item included in the encounter, place the **Standard of the Unyielding Line** here.

The standard begins **unattuned** and **unplanted**.

Possible uses during the demo:

- discover the item before combat;
- identify it;
- plant it during combat;
- demonstrate its AC aura;
- demonstrate anti-forced-movement support;
- expend a charge on Last Line.

For a shorter demo, the DM may instead start with the item already owned by one character.

---

# Optional Interaction — Command the Field

If an Emperor Warlock or another appropriate caster knows **Command the Field**, the encounter is a good place to demonstrate:

- three targets;
- concentration;
- Advance;
- Brace;
- Press;
- Withdraw;
- per-target re-selection each turn.

The fractured ground makes Advance and Withdraw especially visible.

---

# Optional Interaction — Anchor of Command

A character with **Anchor of Command** can use Hold Fast near the eastern tunnel or the expedition platform.

This demonstrates:

- Bonus Action activation;
- speed becoming 0;
- forced-movement immunity;
- temporary HP;
- temporary AC support.

---

# Optional Mid-Campaign Reward

If the party resolves the encounter by:

- protecting others under pressure;
- recognizing the Glassback's motive;
- holding the line during the collapse;
- preventing unnecessary deaths;
- or demonstrating clear battlefield leadership,

the DM may grant:

## Weight of Authority — Tier I: Recognized Presence

Reward:

- +5 maximum HP;
- +1 initiative;
- once per Long Rest, allow self or ally within 10 ft. to reroll a failed save against fear or forced movement.

This should be granted **after the encounter**, directly to an existing character.

No level-up or rebuild should be required.

This is intended to demonstrate Grimoire's mid-campaign reward workflow.

---

# Change-Request Demo Hook

For a live Host/DM/Player demonstration, use this optional sequence after the encounter:

1. The DM grants Weight of Authority — Tier I to a player character.
2. The player submits a permanent character change request related to the reward.
3. The DM reviews the request.
4. The player chooses **Modify** once.
5. The DM sees:
   - original request;
   - modified request;
   - final accepted version.
6. The DM accepts the modified request.
7. The character updates without duplication.

The exact narrative change can be small, for example:

> Rename the reward's displayed title from `Recognized Presence` to `Vault-Hardened Presence` while preserving the mechanics.

This keeps the demo focused on workflow rather than arguing about game balance.

---

# Disconnect / Reconnect Demo Hook

During the live session:

1. Apply a public effect to multiple targets.
2. Apply a secret effect to one target.
3. Disconnect one player.
4. Advance the encounter:
   - reduce one Glassback Pressure state;
   - update one effect;
   - optionally mark one effect Due to End.
5. Reconnect the player.
6. Confirm:
   - current HP restored correctly;
   - public effects restored;
   - secret effect mechanical consequence remains without revealing its identity;
   - no duplicate resources or effects appear.

---

# Public / Secret Effect Demonstration

## Public Multi-Target Effect

Use **Braced** or another visible temporary effect on two or more characters.

End it for only one target first.

Expected state:

- Target A: ENDED
- Target B: ACTIVE or DUE_TO_END

This demonstrates per-target effect lifecycle.

## Secret Effect

Use **Resonant Fracture** or a hidden DM-created effect.

The player may see only the mechanical consequence if appropriate.

The player should not see:

- secret effect identity;
- DM-only trigger condition;
- hidden Collapse Counter.

---

# Encounter Resolution

The encounter can end in several valid ways.

## Kill the Glassback

The Glassback dies and Pressure Collapse triggers.

The body becomes:

- brittle ceramic plates;
- crystallized mineral fluid;
- dense mineral deposits.

## Drive It Deeper

If the party opens the deeper tunnel and stops threatening it, the Glassback retreats.

This is a successful resolution.

## Distract It

If the party uses enough mineral material to redirect it, they may bypass the creature.

This is also a successful resolution.

## Collapse the Vault

If Resonant Fracture reaches 3, the partial collapse changes the battlefield and may force either side to retreat.

The Glassback may immediately prioritize escape over combat.

# Salvage

Possible nonmagical salvage:

- ceramic shell plates;
- crystallized internal fluid;
- mineral nodules;
- pressure-stable crystal structures;
- unusual abrasive slurry residue.

These are intentionally generic.

The encounter does not require a crafting subsystem.

# Minimal 90–120 Second Live Demo Path

For a short creator demo:

1. Open prepared campaign.
2. Show Pressure Vault encounter is prepared but inactive.
3. Host creates session.
4. DM connects existing campaign.
5. Player joins.
6. DM activates Pressure Vault.
7. Show Glassback + public Unstable Pressure.
8. Show secret Resonant Fracture exists only on DM side.
9. Apply Braced to two targets.
10. Mark one target Due to End, then end only that target.
11. Use one visible Glassback action.
12. Disconnect player.
13. Change one live value/effect.
14. Reconnect player and confirm restoration.
15. End encounter.
16. Grant Weight of Authority — Tier I.
17. Submit / Modify / Accept one character change request.
18. Show campaign still exists independently after session ends.
