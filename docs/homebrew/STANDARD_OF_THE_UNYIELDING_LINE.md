# Standard of the Unyielding Line

**Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)**

A heavy military standard reinforced with an unreasonable amount of metal. It is less “carried” than “negotiated with.”

While attuned to the standard and carrying it:

- You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.
- Allies within **10 feet** of you gain **+1 to saving throws** while they can see the standard.

## Plant the Standard

As an **action**, you can plant the standard in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you.

While planted:

- The standard is treated as a fixed object.
- Allies within **15 feet** gain **+1 AC**.
- Allies within **15 feet** have advantage on checks and saving throws made to resist being moved against their will.
- The bearer no longer needs to hold it.

The standard remains planted until you use an action to retrieve it or it is otherwise removed.

## Last Line

The standard has **3 charges**.

When a creature within **15 feet** of the planted standard would be reduced to 0 HP, you can use your **Reaction** and expend 1 charge to cause that creature to drop to **1 HP instead**.

The standard regains **1d3 expended charges at dawn**.
