# Grimoire, for Zellorea

The table decides what is possible. The character sheet software should not.

15 to 30 minutes with one difficult piece of your own content is plenty.

## 2. Try it

Install steps: [INSTALL LINK]

## 3. What I would like you to test
Please read "What Grimoire currently understands mechanically" (zellorea-mechanics.md, one page). Then find something on that page that you have designed and that Grimoire cannot express correctly.

Pick the design you think a generic 5e tool would find hardest, for example Legendary Player Characters, mythic progression, expanded martial mechanics or a full custom class, and try to build it. The failures I most want to hear about:

- Grimoire does not understand this mechanic.
- This technically works, but I have to build it in a ridiculous way.
- This system assumes 5e works in a way that my rules do not.
- This should interact mechanically, but Grimoire can only describe it.

## What you would get if it works
You could send your creations to your players as one file, with everything they depend on included. They import it and use it on their character sheets without extra hassle. They need the app, and for now it is Android only.

## What I would most like back
Short answers are fine.

1. What could you not represent at all?
2. What only worked through an awkward workaround?
3. What did Grimoire model differently from how you naturally think about the mechanic?
4. What took much longer to build than it should have?

None. Your own design is a better test than mine.
