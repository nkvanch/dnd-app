# Braced

**Custom Condition / Effect**

A creature that is **Braced** has committed to holding its position rather than moving freely.

While Braced:

- Its speed is reduced by **10 feet**.
- It gains **+1 AC**.
- It has advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to resist being:
  - shoved
  - dragged
  - pushed
  - pulled
  - knocked prone
  - otherwise moved against its will
- It cannot take the **Dash** action.

The effect ends if:

- the source that applied it ends the effect;
- the creature becomes incapacitated;
- or a feature specifically says Braced ends.

---

## Demo Application Action

### Take a Brace

As a **Bonus Action**, you become Braced until the start of your next turn.

This action is only a convenient demo/test source for the effect; it does not need to be part of the universal Braced condition itself.

## Grimoire Representation

Automate where supported:

- speed -10 ft.
- AC +1
- advantage against forced movement
- advantage against being knocked prone
- Dash disabled
- effect duration and expiration

Potential reminder/table resolution:

- unusual forced-movement cases that do not map cleanly to a structured category
