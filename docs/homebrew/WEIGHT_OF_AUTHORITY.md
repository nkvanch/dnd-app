# Weight of Authority

**Mid-Campaign Reward Feature**

A character who has endured decisive battles, carried responsibility for others, and repeatedly become the person others instinctively look toward in moments of crisis develops a presence that is difficult to ignore.

This is a **mid-campaign reward feature**, not a feat. It can be granted directly by the DM and upgraded later without requiring a level-up, respec, or character rebuild.

---

## Tier I — Recognized Presence

You gain:

- **+5 maximum HP**
- **+1 to initiative**

Once per Long Rest, when you or an ally within **10 feet** fails a saving throw against being frightened or being moved against their will, you can allow that creature to reroll the saving throw.

The new roll must be used.

---

## Tier II — Proven Commander

This tier includes all Tier I benefits, improved as follows:

- maximum HP bonus increases to **+10**
- initiative bonus increases to **+2**
- the reroll feature can be used **twice per Long Rest**
- its range increases to **20 feet**

In addition:

> When initiative is rolled, choose yourself or one ally within 20 feet who can see or hear you. That creature gains temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier.

If Charisma is not appropriate for the character, the DM may allow Constitution or Wisdom instead when the reward is granted.

---

## Tier III — Weight of Command

This tier includes all lower-tier benefits, improved again:

- maximum HP bonus increases to **+15**
- initiative bonus increases to **+3**
- the reroll feature can be used **three times per Long Rest**
- its range increases to **30 feet**

The initiative-triggered temporary hit points remain.

### Stand With Me

Once per Long Rest, when you or an ally within 30 feet would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your **Reaction** to cause that creature to drop to **1 hit point instead**.

After using Stand With Me, allies of your choice within 10 feet of that creature gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.

---

## Upgrade Rules

The tiers **replace**, rather than stack with, previous tiers.

Examples:

- Tier I grants +5 maximum HP total.
- Tier II grants +10 maximum HP total, not +15.
- Tier III grants +15 maximum HP total, not +30.

The same replacement rule applies to:

- initiative bonus
- reroll uses
- reroll range

This keeps the reward mechanically clean and avoids duplicate stacking.

---

## Grimoire Representation

This reward is designed to demonstrate:

- adding a permanent feature mid-campaign
- upgrading a feature without rebuilding the character
- provenance/history from Tier I → Tier II → Tier III
- permanent maximum-HP modification
- permanent initiative modification
- limited-use resources
- ally-targeted rerolls
- range growth
- initiative-triggered temporary HP
- a Reaction that prevents a creature from dropping to 0 HP

Where possible, later tiers should replace the previous tier as an authoritative upgrade rather than layering duplicate modifiers.
