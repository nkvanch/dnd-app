# Grimoire, for Lee

15 to 30 minutes with one difficult piece of your own content is plenty.

## 1. Watch first
[DEMO LINK, 60 seconds: one small themed pack across five content types]

## 2. Try it
Android build, free and open source, works offline and needs no account or external server: https://github.com/nkvanch/Grimoire/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha
Install steps: https://nkvanch.github.io/Grimoire/documentation.html#install

## 3. What I would like you to test
You work across spells, creatures, items, feats, subclasses and classes, so you are a good test of whether Grimoire stays consistent across content types. Pick whichever piece of your own content is most likely to cause trouble and build it. I am not asking whether it worked, I am asking:

- Was there something you wanted the mechanic to do that Grimoire could not express?
- Did the software force you into a structure you would not naturally use?
- Could you build it, but only through an awkward workaround?
- Did the resulting character or monster actually behave the way you intended?

## What you would get if it works
One place to build spells, creatures, items and feats together, and a way to send them to other people as a single file that they import in one step. They need the app, and for now it is Android only.

## What I would most like back
Short answers are fine.

1. What could you not represent at all?
2. What only worked through an awkward workaround?
3. What did Grimoire model differently from how you naturally think about the mechanic?
4. What took much longer to build than it should have?

## 4. Optional sample
stormbound-test-pack.grimoire-pack is a small original pack: a feat, a spell, a weapon, a creature and a condition that share one storm theme. The weapon and the creature both apply the condition. It is not meant to impress, only to show how several content types behave together. Import it, try it, then replace pieces with your own ideas. Everything in it is labelled (Demo).
