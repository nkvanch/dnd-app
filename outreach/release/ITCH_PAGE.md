Page body:

> ## Grimoire (alpha)
> Build the character you actually want, including homebrew that does not fit a standard character builder, and keep it on your own device. Free, open source (GPL-3.0-or-later), works offline, and requires no account or external server. Android only for now.
>
> What it does: character sheets, homebrew builders for classes, subclasses, species, backgrounds, feats, spells, items, monsters and conditions that use the same systems as the built-in content, a test button in the builders, shareable .grimoire-pack files, and local network campaign sync.
>
> What it does not do: resolve reactive features or hit and miss outcomes automatically (it shows them and you decide), or run non-5e rulesets.
>
> Install: download the APK and follow the steps below the download. It is a sideloaded alpha, so Android will ask for permission and Play Protect may show a warning.
>
> Feedback wanted: where does it get in your way?
>
> Contains only SRD 5.1 (CC-BY-4.0) and original content. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
